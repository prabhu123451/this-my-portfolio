Case study 01 · Mobile app

GrowPlant

A plant-care app that helps beginners water, feed and place their plants correctly, so fewer plants die and caring for them feels easy.

View case study ↗ See the process
Duration4 weeks
RoleUI/UX Designer
PlatformMobile app
ToolsFigma
GrowPlant cover
01

Problem & solution

The problem

Beginners don't know what their plants need.

Care advice is scattered across videos, blogs and labels. New plant owners forget to water, over-water or place plants in the wrong light, and most give up after losing a plant.

  • No single place for watering and light needs
  • Easy to forget a care schedule
  • Hard to tell what is wrong with a sick plant
The solution

One simple app that tells you what to do and when.

GrowPlant gives every plant its own profile with a clear care plan, smart reminders and quick tips, all in a clean interface that takes seconds to use.

  • Plant profile with water, light and soil info
  • Reminders that fit your routine
  • Simple tips when a plant looks unhealthy
02

Design process

  1. 01

    Research

    Interviewed 6 plant owners and studied 3 competitor apps to learn why beginners lose their plants.

    3 days
  2. 02

    Define

    Turned research into a persona, pain points and a clear problem statement.

    2 days
  3. 03

    User flow

    Mapped the path from adding a plant to getting a care reminder in the fewest steps.

    2 days
  4. 04

    Wireframe

    Sketched low-fidelity screens in Figma to test structure before adding any style.

    4 days
  5. 05

    UI design

    Built the final visuals: colour, type, icons and a reusable component set.

    7 days
  6. 06

    Prototype

    Linked every screen into a clickable mobile prototype in Figma.

    3 days
  7. 07

    Test

    Ran usability tests with 5 users, then fixed the issues they found.

    3 days
Total project duration24 days · 4 weeks
